I've kept talking about command-driven and event-sourced apps in Ruby, to anyone who'll listen.

I was on the Dead Code podcast talking about [my WIP Ruby stack](/posts/2026-05-wip/).

Listen to the podcast [here](https://shows.acast.com/dead-code/episodes/twilight-of-the-object-god-with-ismael-celis) (or on your preferred app).

I also spoke (again) at the July 2026 edition of the London Ruby User Group. This one was about [Sidereal](https://github.com/ismasan/sidereal), my Ruby framework for building command-driven, reactive apps. It's the same subject as the one at [Wroclove.rb](/posts/2026-04-wrocloverb-haggis-ruby/) back in April, but I explore a bit more all that you get by just making commands the authoritative entry point into your apps.
